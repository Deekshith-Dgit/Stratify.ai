# STRATIFY AI — BLUEPRINT V1

Your Personalized, Psychology-Inspired Career & Life Mentor

---

🌟 VISION

Stratify AI is an open-source AI mentor designed to help students and young professionals make smarter, more informed decisions about their careers, education, and life goals.

Unlike traditional career quizzes or generic AI assistants, Stratify AI aims to understand the individual before providing guidance. By combining psychology-inspired profiling, personal goals, strengths, weaknesses, constraints, and real-world realities, the platform delivers honest, realistic, and actionable mentorship.

«Not just motivation. Better decisions.»

---

🔍 PROBLEM STATEMENT

Millions of students and young professionals face uncertainty about:

- Career choices
- Competitive exams
- College applications
- Skill development
- Long-term goals

Most existing solutions provide generic advice and fail to consider:

- Individual personality
- Learning style
- Discipline and habits
- Family and financial constraints
- Risk tolerance
- Personal motivations

As a result, users often develop unrealistic expectations, inefficient plans, and poor decision-making frameworks.

Stratify AI aims to solve this through personalized, context-aware, and realistic guidance.

🧭 CORE PHILOSOPHY

Stratify AI follows five principles:

1. Understand Before Advising

The system should understand the user before making recommendations.

1. Detect Blind Spots

The AI should identify contradictions between goals, habits, expectations, and reality.

1. Challenge Unrealistic Assumptions

The platform should provide honest feedback instead of false reassurance.

1. Stay Grounded in Reality

Roadmaps should be realistic, practical, and achievable.

1. Evolve With The User

Guidance should improve as more information and progress data become available.

«Think of Stratify AI as a mentor, not a search engine.»

🔄 USER FLOW

Step 1: User Type Selection

The user identifies themselves as:

- Student
- Professional
- Other

Step 2: Purpose Selection

The user selects what they need help with:

- Career Exploration
- Exam Preparation
- College Applications
- Skill Development
- Life Decisions
- Open Mentor Chat

Step 3: Adaptive Onboarding

The system asks dynamic questions based on:

- User type
- Goals
- Previous answers

No two users are required to follow the exact same onboarding flow.

Step 4: AI Profile Generation

The system generates a profile including:

- Strengths
- Weaknesses
- Motivations
- Learning style
- Preferences
- Constraints

Step 5: Blind Spot Detection

The AI identifies contradictions and unrealistic assumptions.

Step 6: Personalized Roadmap

The user receives:

- Action plan
- Milestones
- Resources
- Timelines
- Risks
- Alternative paths

Step 7: Ongoing Mentorship

The user can continue interacting with the mentor and update progress over time.
